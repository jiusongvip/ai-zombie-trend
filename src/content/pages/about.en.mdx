## What this is

AI Zombie Trend is an online generator for the viral "zombie love story" short —
a 15-second, scored, four-shot film built from two of your photos. Upload one
photo of the survivor and one of the turned, pick a story mode (couple, pet,
friends or family), and the workbench writes the prompts, orders the shots and
adds the soundtrack. The result is a watermark-free MP4 sized for TikTok,
Reels and Shorts.

## How the video is made

Frames are rendered by third-party text-to-video models integrated through our
platform. We handle prompt construction, shot continuity, grading and audio so
you never touch a prompt box. Rendering costs credits — one-time packs with no
subscription — and a failed render returns your credits automatically.

## Our principles

- **Consent first.** Use only photos of yourself, your own pet, or people who
  have agreed to appear. The generator asks you to confirm this before every
  render, and we block content involving minors or public figures.
- **Nothing is public by default.** Finished films stay in your library unless
  you tick the opt-in that shares one to the public example wall.
- **Synthetic media is labeled.** Every shared clip carries an "AI generated"
  badge on the page — viewers should never have to guess.
- **Your photos are input, not inventory.** They are sent to the video model
  to make your film and are not sold or repurposed. Details live in the
  [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## Who is behind it

AI Zombie Trend is an independent developer project, not a media studio. The
same person builds the tool, writes the guides in the blog, and answers the
support mail — and yes, renders their own zombie films with it.
