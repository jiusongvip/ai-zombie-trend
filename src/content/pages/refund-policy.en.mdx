We sell one thing: credit packs. You buy credits, you spend them on renders, and nothing renews on its own — there are no subscriptions to cancel. This page says exactly what happens to your money in each situation.

## Credits returned automatically

A render that does not deliver you a film costs you nothing. Credits are reserved when the render starts and are returned to your balance by the system, without you asking, when:

- the generation fails at the provider,
- the finished video is blocked by our content review, or
- the task is cancelled or expires without producing a film.

You can confirm this in your own library: a failed job shows as failed, and the credits are back in your balance. This is the most common refund situation on this service and it is fully automated.

## Money refunds

**Unused packs, 7 days.** If you bought a credit pack and have not spent any of its credits, email us within 7 days of the purchase and we will refund the full amount to the card or account you paid with.

**Paid but never delivered.** If a purchase did not credit your balance at all — a dropped webhook, a double charge, anything where you paid and received nothing — we refund it in full once we have confirmed it. Send us the date and the amount and we will find the transaction.

**Processing time.** Approved refunds are issued within 5 business days. Depending on your card issuer, the amount can then take a few more days to appear on your statement.

## What we do not refund

- **Credits already spent on a film that was delivered.** You received what you paid for. We do not refund because a render did not match a taste or expectation — the credit cost of each model is shown before you submit, and you can preview the example wall first.
- **Credits you chose not to use after the 7-day window.** They do not expire, so nothing is lost; they are simply no longer refundable as cash.
- **Purchases made in breach of our AI-Generated Content Usage Policy**, including accounts terminated for it. This does not affect refunds you are legally entitled to.

## Disputes and chargebacks

Please write to us first at **jiusongvip@gmail.com** — a refund request is usually settled the same day, while a bank dispute takes weeks and freezes the money on both sides. If you did file a dispute, we will respond to it with the order record, the delivery status, and this policy.

## Contact

Refund requests: **jiusongvip@gmail.com**. Include your account email and the purchase date; the order number from your settings page helps but is not required.
