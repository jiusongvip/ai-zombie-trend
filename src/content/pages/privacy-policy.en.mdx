Last updated: October 10, 2026.

The short version: we keep your email, your account records, the two photos while
they are being rendered, and the films you make. Photos leave our storage within
24 hours. Your card number never reaches us, we do not sell your data, and we do
not train models on you.

## What we collect

- **Account** — your email address, the name you give us, a hashed password, and
  one record per sign-in session (IP address, the browser's user-agent string,
  and the time). Sessions exist so an unfamiliar sign-in can be spotted.
- **Your photos** — the two images you upload for a render.
- **Your films** — the generated video, plus the style, duration, aspect ratio and
  scene text needed to produce it, so you can re-run or edit it later.
- **Orders** — amount, currency, the payment provider, that provider's
  confirmation record, and the billing email it attaches to the transaction.
- **Usage** — anonymous page views, described under Analytics.

We do not ask for, and do not receive: your card number, CVV or expiry date
(those stay with the payment processor), your contacts, your photo library beyond
the two files you choose, or anything else on your device.

## Your photos and your films

This is the part people actually ask about, so here is exactly what happens to the
two photos you upload.

**Stripped before they leave your device.** The generator re-encodes each photo in
your browser before uploading it. The bytes we receive are a flattened JPEG, so
EXIF metadata — GPS coordinates, device model, the original file name — never
reaches our servers. If you upload through another surface, strip that metadata
yourself first.

**Content-addressed.** Uploads land in storage we control — Cloudflare R2 or S3
when configured, otherwise our own application server. The stored filename derives
from a hash of the image content, not from your account or your original file
name, so there is no list of usernames to walk.

**Rendered by one provider, for one job.** To produce your film, the two photos
are passed to the video model provider — ByteDance Seedance, called through fal —
for that single generation. That is the only reason they leave our servers. They
are screened for content on the way in, and the finished film is screened again
before it is handed back to you.

**Deleted within 24 hours.** The photos you stage are removed from storage
automatically within 24 hours, whether the render finished, failed, or was never
started — every upload is entered in a retention ledger, and a sweep deletes the
object when its window closes.

**Yours until you remove it.** A finished film appears only in your own library. It
reaches the public example wall only if you tick the sharing option when you
submit the render — that option is off by default, and nothing about your likeness
is published without it. Films do not expire on their own; deleting one hides it
from your account immediately, and the order record stays for accounting. If you
want the rendered files purged from storage as well, email us below and we will
remove them.

**No model training.** Your photos and your films are not used to train any model,
ours or anyone else's.

**One thing we ask of you.** Upload faces you have permission to use — your own, or
someone who has agreed. Before a photo-driven render starts, you confirm that
declaration on the form, and we record the version of the wording you accepted
against that render.

## Who processes your data

Each of these receives the least it needs to do its job, under its own terms:

| Processor | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare | Your upload bytes and the finished film; request metadata for hosting | Application hosting and object storage |
| fal — ByteDance Seedance | Your two photos plus the direction for the scene | Rendering the film |
| SeeAPI | Your photos, your scene text, and the finished film | Content screening |

## Cookies and local storage

Our own site uses a session cookie so you stay signed in, plus small preferences
(language and theme). Umami's script may set a first-party cookie to count a
visitor once rather than many times. There are no advertising cookies, no
cross-site trackers, and no third-party pixels on this site.

## Analytics

Page views go to a self-hosted Umami instance we operate, not to an advertising
network. It records which page was viewed, where the visitor came from, and rough
device and country information. It does not receive your email, your prompts, your
photo or film URLs, or any private identifier, and it keeps no IP address. Google
Analytics is not loaded on this site; if that ever changes, this page will say so
and you will be asked before any analytics cookie is set.

## Email

We send email only when you ask for it: an account-verification link and a
password-reset link. There is no newsletter, no marketing, and no reminder about
an unfinished checkout.

## Children

This service is not directed at children. We do not knowingly accept accounts or
photographs from anyone under 16 (or under the age of digital consent in your
country, if that is higher). A photograph of a child can be used only by a parent
or guardian who has the right to consent for them; if you believe a child's data
has reached us, email us and we will remove the account and its material.

## Where your data lives

Our application and storage run on Cloudflare's global network, with the origin
configured in the United States. The processors named above operate in their own
regions and rely on their own transfer mechanisms; we send each of them the
minimum required for the step it performs.

## Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Provider credentials saved in the admin
panel are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Passwords are hashed. The likeness
declaration and the content screen are enforced on the server, so neither can be
skipped by a modified client, and photo screening fails closed — an unchecked
photo is never rendered. Generation and upload endpoints are rate-limited, and
those counters live in memory rather than in the database. Administrative access
is role-based and limited to the people who need it.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your personal information from your
account settings, and you can ask us to erase the rendered files as well. You may
also object to processing, ask for a copy of your data in a portable form, and
withdraw a consent declaration you previously gave us — withdrawing it does not
affect renders already made under it.

Email us below to exercise any of this. We may need to confirm it is your account
before we act, and we answer requests within 30 days. If you are in the EU, the
UK, or Switzerland, you can also raise a complaint with your local data protection
authority; elsewhere, with your national consumer or data regulator.
