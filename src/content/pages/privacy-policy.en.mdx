## Your Photos and Your Films

This is the part people actually ask about, so here is exactly what happens to the two photos you upload.

**Where they are stored.** Uploads land in storage we control — Cloudflare R2 or S3 when configured, otherwise our own application server. The stored filename is derived from a hash of the image content, not from your account or your original file name.

**Stored as you sent them.** We keep the original bytes rather than re-encoding the file, so metadata travels with it. A photo exported from a phone can carry GPS coordinates and device details — check and strip that before uploading.

**Uploading before you sign up.** You can stage both photos and pick a story without an account; only the render itself requires one. Every upload must be an image, is capped at 10 MB per file, and requests are rate-limited. Your staged pair survives the sign-up round-trip, so you never re-upload.

**Who else sees them.** To produce your film, the two photos are passed to the video model provider — ByteDance Seedance, called through fal — for that single generation. That is the only reason they leave our servers.

**We do not train on you.** Your photos and your finished films are not used to train any model, ours or anyone else's.

**Who can view the result.** A finished film appears only in your own library. Nothing is published to the public example wall unless you send it to us and we agree to feature it.

**Keeping and removing.** There is no automatic expiry: staged uploads and finished films are kept until you ask us to remove them. Deleting a film from your library hides it from your account immediately; the transaction record stays for accounting. If you want the underlying photos and renders purged from storage as well, email us using the address below and we will remove them.

**One thing we ask of you.** Upload faces you have permission to use — your own, or someone who has agreed. It is the same rule that keeps your film publishable on TikTok or Reels.
